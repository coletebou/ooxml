features:
  - title: Free-form pages
    details: Click anywhere on a page to type. Text containers, pictures and files sit where you put them; drag one by its grip, resize it from its corner, or drop and paste pictures and files.
  - title: Rich text and to-dos
    details: Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough and highlight, three heading levels, bullet and numbered lists, links, and to-do tags gathered in one To Do pane.
  - title: Ink
    details: Draw with a pen or a highlighter in six and four colours and three widths; the eraser removes whole strokes. Ink is part of the page and syncs like the text.
  - title: Office files on the page
    details: Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Visio files are shown live and read-only by the suite's own viewers; any other file is a card you can open or download.
  - title: Local first
    details: Notebooks live in this browser's IndexedDB. No server is needed, and tabs of one browser share a notebook in real time over BroadcastChannel.
  - title: Bring your own sync server
    details: Point it at any y-websocket server (the OpenTeams reference server's /sync works) and people on other machines edit the same notebook, with presence.
